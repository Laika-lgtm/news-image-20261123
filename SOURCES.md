## Heroes

1. **COP31 collapses — no deal (UN News).** COP31 closed Sunday
   without a final climate pact after two weeks of talks and two days
   of overtime — the longest UN climate summit on record, and the
   first to end with no cover decision since the 2000 Hague COP.
   COP31 President Murat Kurum: "I understand that there is no
   agreement on this text. For that reason, these decisions have not
   been adopted." The EU, UK, Panama, Marshall Islands and Norway
   refused a text with no fossil-fuel mention; the $120bn adaptation
   target and the 35% electricity goal both fell with the pact.
   UN Secretary-General António Guterres: "deeply disappointed".
   Focus shifts to COP32, Addis Ababa, November 2027. Tag REPORTED:
   carried by the day's wires (UN News, 23 Nov).
2. **15 killed in Kyiv region; Dymer toll 11 (Ukrinform, Reuters).**
   Russian overnight strikes killed 15 people — 14 in the Kyiv
   region and one in Zaporizhzhia city (Al Jazeera, Monday morning).
   In Dymer, a strike partly collapsed a five-storey block; the toll
   rose to 11 after rescuers recovered another body early Monday
   (Kyiv regional head Tymur Tkachenko, via Telegram; Ukrinform).
   A seven-year-old boy, Davyd, was pulled alive after 14 hours
   under the rubble. Vyshneve: 3 killed, 15 hurt when a drone hit a
   store. Bila Tserkva: 1 killed. Ukraine's air defences neutralised
   134 of 153 Russian drones launched overnight (Ukrinform, Monday).
   Tag REPORTED: Ukrainian Air Force / Ukrinform wires.
3. **Iran vows 'every means' against US blockade (Euronews).**
   Foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei on Monday called the
   US naval blockade "military aggression" and said Tehran "will use
   every means to counter US aggression", a day after President Trump
   said Iranian ships that try to breach it would go "to the bottom
   of the sea". IRGC-linked Tasnim said no official body had
   announced a tanker-convoy plan. Qatar's prime minister was due to
   meet Secretary of State Marco Rubio on Monday as Doha mediates.
   Tag CLAIM: one side's account, labelled as such.

## Cards

4. **Ukraine says sea drones sank a Russian warship (Guardian).**
   Ukraine confirmed Monday it launched seven domestically made
   naval drones from a mothership in Sunday's strike that sank the
   Russian corvette Merkury at Libya's Benghazi naval base; a Ka-27
   helicopter was destroyed with her and the dry cargo ship MV
   Angara was hit. Libya's eastern government filed a complaint with
   the UN General Assembly and Security Council.
5. **New Zealand hang on for a draw after India's 720/9 (Cricinfo).**
   1st Test, Basin Reserve, Wellington, ended in a draw on Monday
   23 November: New Zealand 368 and 239/5 (Conway 65, Mitchell 49*,
   Phillips 26*) drew with India 720/9 declared — the highest Test
   total on New Zealand soil (Rahul 136, Pant 128). Mitchell and
   Phillips batted out 120 overs. Player of the match: Henry
   Nicholls (175*).
6. **NATO meets as Poland names all three missiles (Reuters).**
   NATO's North Atlantic Council met in Brussels on Monday over
   three Russian objects that entered Polish airspace on 20 Nov.
   Polish prosecutors: all three were Kh-101 cruise missiles,
   manufactured in the fourth quarter of 2026 at a plant in Dubna.
   Secretary General Mark Rutte (21 Nov, Warsaw): NATO will defend
   "every inch" of Polish territory.
7. **Democrats reach 258 House seats (NBC News).** Democrat Chuck
   Hubbard defeated longtime Republican Rep. Virginia Foxx in
   North Carolina's 5th District after a recount — by 330 votes out
   of about 287,000 cast — giving Democrats at least 258 House
   seats, their largest majority since 1992. Four races still
   uncalled.
8. **Candy-factory fire toll rises to 24 (Reuters).** Indonesia
   confirmed 24 deaths in the fire at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia
   candy factory in Cikande, west of Jakarta, with about 30 people
   still missing. Preliminary reports point to an air-conditioner
   short circuit; identifying victims by DNA could take two to
   three weeks.
9. **Armenia breaks ground on the 'Trump Route' (Anadolu).**
   Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan on Monday announced the launch of
   the Trump Route for International Peace and Prosperity (TRIPP),
   calling it "the largest economic project in Armenia since the
   country gained its independence", as preparatory construction
   began in Syunik. President Trump announced the TRIPP Development
   Company and invited Pashinyan to the White House next year.
10. **Zverev ends the year as world No. 1 (LaPresse).** Alexander
    Zverev ended the season as world number one for the first time —
    two Grand Slam titles, 69 wins. Carlos Alcaraz won the Nitto ATP
    Finals final in Turin, 6–2, 7–6(7) over Zverev; at 23 Alcaraz is
    the youngest to hold a title at every Grand Slam and the Finals.
    Jannik Sinner finished second after a knee injury ended his
    season early.
11. **Giddey rewrites a 60-year Bulls record (ABC Australia).**
    Australian Josh Giddey recorded 23 points, 14 rebounds and 22
    assists for the Chicago Bulls against Denver — a triple-double no
    Chicago player had managed since 1966. (ABC, 23 Nov 07:58 UTC.)
12. **Kremlin calls Baltic ship strike 'terrorism' (Reuters).**
    The Kremlin on Monday accused Ukraine of "terrorist activity"
    after drones struck the Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the
    Baltic on Saturday; the vessel is being towed back to Russian
    territory. All 16 crew were evacuated to a nearby Russian ship;
    one was slightly injured. Finland's military says Ukraine was
    likely behind the strike.
13. **Russia 'plans a nuclear-armed satellite' (dpa, via Just
    Security).** German media reported on Monday that NATO members'
    intelligence services have concluded Russia plans to launch a
    satellite armed with a nuclear weapon into space this year.
