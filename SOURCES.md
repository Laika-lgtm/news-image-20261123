## Heroes

1. **Russian strikes kill 15 in Kyiv region (Ukrinform, Reuters).**
   Overnight 22→23 Nov, Russia fired 153 drones at Ukraine; 134
   were downed or jammed (Ukrainian Air Force, Monday). In the Kyiv
   region 15 were killed: 11 in Dymer, where a strike partly
   collapsed a five-storey block (regional head Tymur Tkachenko,
   Telegram, Monday); 3 at a supermarket in Vyshneve; 1 at a home in
   Bila Tserkva. A seven-year-old boy was pulled alive after 14
   hours under the rubble. Note: the Kyiv administration gave 14
   killed on Monday morning; the toll rose to 15 during the day
   (11+3+1, all Kyiv region). The often-quoted "186 drones" was the
   previous night (22 Nov), not this raid. Tag REPORTED.
2. **Iran vows 'every means' against US blockade (Euronews).**
   Foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei on Monday called the
   US naval blockade "military aggression" and said Tehran "will
   use every means to counter US aggression", a day after President
   Trump said Iranian ships that try to breach it would go "to the
   bottom of the sea". Tag CLAIM: one side's account, labelled.
3. **Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as chief executive (Reuters).**
   Nvidia's board voted unanimously on Monday 23 November to
   reinstate Jensen Huang as chief executive, 11 days after he
   stepped down. Shares jumped 31.5% on Monday (+$952bn market
   value); the Nasdaq gained 7%, the SOX 14%, the S&P 500 $2.8tn.
   Trump said the return would not have happened without his
   intervention. Tag REPORTED.

## Cards

4. **Zelenskyy: sea drones sank Russian warship (Guardian).**
   The corvette Merkury was sunk off Benghazi on Sunday; on Monday
   Zelenskyy confirmed it and the SBU released video of seven
   drones hitting the ship. The Monday confirmation + video are the
   story, not the Sunday sinking.
5. **New Zealand hang on for a draw (Cricinfo).** 1st Test, Basin
   Reserve, Wellington, drawn on Monday 23 November: New Zealand
   368 and 239/5 drew with India 720/9 declared. Mitchell and
   Phillips batted out 120 overs.
6. **NATO sends AWACS forward after Poland (NATO).** NATO's
   spokesperson said on Monday that the Supreme Allied Commander
   Europe authorised the forward deployment of AWACS aircraft after
   Russian missiles came down in Poland last week; alliance
   officials discussed it in Brussels on Monday. Poland confirmed
   the three objects found were Kh-101 cruise missiles.
7. **Oxfam: 99% of Gaza is now toxic (Oxfam).** "Environmental War
   Crimes", published Monday 23 November by Oxfam and the Conflict
   and Environment Observatory: indicators of potential
   contamination across more than 99% of Gaza; 78% of land
   affected; 85% of water infrastructure damaged or destroyed;
   about 18 litres of water per person per day.
8. **Candy-factory fire toll rises to 24 (Reuters).** Indonesia
   confirmed 24 deaths in the fire at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia
   candy factory in Cikande, with about 30 still missing; toll
   confirmed Monday 05:57 UTC. DNA checks may take weeks.
9. **Armenia breaks ground on the 'Trump Route' (Anadolu).** Prime
   Minister Nikol Pashinyan on Monday announced the launch of the
   Trump Route for International Peace and Prosperity (TRIPP),
   "the largest economic project in Armenia since the country
   gained its independence".
10. **Zverev ends the year as world No. 1 (LaPresse).** Alexander
    Zverev ended the season as world number one for the first time
    — two Grand Slam titles, 69 wins. Carlos Alcaraz won the Nitto
    ATP Finals final in Turin.
11. **Giddey rewrites a 60-year Bulls record (ABC Australia).**
    Josh Giddey: 23 points, 14 rebounds, 22 assists for the Chicago
    Bulls against Denver; the game ended ~02:30 UTC Monday, reported
    by ABC 23 Nov 07:58 UTC.
12. **Kremlin calls Baltic ship strike 'terrorism' (Reuters).**
    The Kremlin on Monday accused Ukraine of "terrorist activity"
    after drones struck the Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the
    Baltic; all 16 crew safe.
13. **Russia 'ready to launch a nuclear satellite' (Reuters).**
    Western intelligence shared among NATO allies warns Russia could
    launch a nuclear-armed satellite, Sputnik-S, as early as December
    2026; German outlets raised the alarm on Monday 23 November.
    Moscow denies it. Note: the satellite has NOT launched — the
    story is the warning, not a launch.
